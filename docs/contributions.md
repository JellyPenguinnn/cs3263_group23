## Use of AI tools

We used Codex and Claude Code throughout. Tea Li Hao operated Codex and committed the work for the team, so commit authorship does not reflect individual contributions. From our specifications, these tools wrote most of the code, tests and experiment scripts, and drafted or revised much of the report and slide text. The research question, formulation, evaluation design, model choice, the generation-first scope after TA feedback and the interpretation of results came from our discussions; we reviewed the AI outputs, iterated on fixes and validation with the tools, and approved the final report and presentation. The same statement appears in Section 9 of the [report](submission-report.md).

Four construction sweeps have three levels; the development pilot uses two families per cell, while the compact/target parameters allow four/ten only after the actual size decision. SAT/UNSAT alternate within every cell, and three repair events rotate evenly. Windows, durations, assignments and interactions stay inside schema v1. Preferences compete; fixed-event contradictions use resource collisions, deadline contradictions use precedence, and blocked UNSAT uses unary domain wipeout. Binary contradiction candidates with an already-empty unary domain are rejected. These mechanisms and coupled covariates limit generalization; the sweeps are not causal interventions.

The builder saves every inspected original/updated specification and every attempted reference, including rejected/unresolved candidates. Only proven labels/optima enter the selected packet. A bounded proposal count, per-solve limit and wall budget prevent unbounded searches. Quota failure remains explicit and cannot turn into silent relabelling. Structural overlaps with development or selected families are rejected. Human gold/text review remains pending. Final mode requires a prior matching policy/source/registry/seed record and actual policy/model decision references; technical reference strings do not establish that a human approved them.
